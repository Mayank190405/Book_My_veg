# Remediation sequence after readiness approval

This is a proposal; no application or schema change is included in the Readiness Pack.

1. Revalidate defects on the approved frozen source/build/schema baseline. Define independent reference balances from verified receipt evidence, including the unresolved ₹1,629.55 case. Historical paid flags are reconciliation evidence, not permission to collect money again.
2. Implement provider-specific verification with official signing contracts, raw-body evidence handling, exact request/merchant/provider identity, amount/currency/customer/purpose matching, fail-closed signatures and verification-unknown timeouts. Remove production mock-success paths. Frontend responses request verification only.
3. Review additive intent/receipt/event/allocation/unapplied-credit/refund/audit models and migration strategy. Inventory existing duplicates before proposing legacy uniqueness. Obtain migration approval separately; do not apply migrations as part of source implementation.
4. Establish one transaction-safe posting service. Conserve verified money across allocations and unapplied amounts; enforce durable provider-event/receipt identity and concurrency-safe retries. All webhook/callback/frontend/sync paths converge on it.
5. Route POS/driver/account cash, electronic tenders and wallet use through that service. Preserve per-slice tender attribution, cap allocations, record excess, reject insufficient wallet funds and make new-order/old-dues workflow atomic. Require durable request identity for retries/edits. Staff-entered electronic amounts are not verified receipts.
6. Separate financial from delivery state; retain partial COD obligations. Replace unsupported paid toggles with explicit receipt/authorized adjustment and reconciliation-required states. Preserve historical evidence; add signed provider-aware partial/full refunds and reversals. Replace destructive cleanup with previewable, evidence-preserving corrections.
7. Use one scoped receivable projection for ledger headline, dues, invoices and reminders, including credits/refunds/opening balances. Reconcile caches independently of existing paid flags. Keep automatic due reminders disabled. Align receipt-time cash reporting and explicit timezone boundaries.
8. Validate only against isolated staging and sandbox fixtures, including forged/missing signatures, provider errors, duplicate/reordered/concurrent events, two/three/many tender slices, partial/overflow settlement, wallet replay/overspend/concurrent deposits, POS edit retry, partial COD delivery, cancellation/refund, legacy paid-without-receipt, cross-store/date reports and old-invoice cash collection. Assert conserved money, no duplicate receipt, no overdraft and unchanged historical evidence.
9. Deliver source diff, migration proposal, tests, reconciliation preview, rollout/rollback procedure and remaining risks for review. Obtain separate explicit approvals before production deployment, actual schema migration, historical corrections or irreversible operations. Do not push to main as a surrogate for deployment approval.
